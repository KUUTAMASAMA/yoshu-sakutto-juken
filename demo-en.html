LESSON 4

Why Sleep Matters for Learning

Many students cut down on sleep to make more time for studying. However, research suggests that this may not be a wise choice. While we are asleep, the brain sorts through the information we have taken in during the day and stores the important parts as long-term memories. In one well-known type of experiment, people who slept after learning a list of words remembered more of them the next day than people who stayed awake. In other words, sleep is not wasted time; it is part of the learning process itself. If you want to remember what you study, it might be better to go to bed a little earlier tonight.

■ 大事な文法：関係代名詞の省略
